How these are worked out
  • First usable draft: the first submit where no piece was blocked, timed from when the task was created.
  • Pastes: answers pasted back into the task (fixing the split by hand is not a new paste).
  • Blocked on first paste: the first time a piece was submitted, a fact problem or a brand or channel rule stopped it.
  • Waiting for approval: in the calendar's history of the piece, from sent for review to approved.
  • Reviewer edits: new versions of the text saved while the piece was waiting for review.
  • Facts changed after approval: an approved piece sent back to draft because a fact it used changed.
  • Read every minute from the task bridge (88) and the calendar (19); the newest {{ max_tasks }} tasks.
